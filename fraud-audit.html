Case Study: Model Risk Audit

Mobile Money Fraud Detection Audit

A technical deep-dive into bias, explainability, and regulatory alignment for automated fraud detection systems in emerging markets.

Scope of Engagement

  • Lifecycle Governance: Assessment from data ingestion to model retirement.
  • Fairness Metrics: Evaluating Demographic Parity and Equalized Odds across balance tiers.
  • Explainability: SHAP-based feature importance mapping to identify "Black Box" risks.
  • Error Analysis: Quantifying the financial and reputational impact of False Positives.

Governance Frameworks

NIST AI RMF 1.0 EU AI ACT (High-Risk) BANK OF GHANA CISD

Key Insights

  • High performance on velocity-based fraud indicators (ROC-AUC 0.94).
  • Observed bias in balance-dependent behavioral features.
  • Fairness risk classified as Material under current Bank of Ghana guidelines.
  • SHAP values reveal over-reliance on a single proxy feature, increasing model drift risk.
