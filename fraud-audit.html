Audit Overview

Fraud Detection AI Model Audit

Independent governance and risk evaluation of a digital payments fraud detection system, assessing model behavior, bias, and regulatory alignment across its full lifecycle.

What This Audit Covers

This audit evaluates the integrity and reliability of a machine learning fraud detection model, focusing on how decisions are made, where risks exist, and whether outcomes are fair across users.

  • End-to-end lifecycle assessment (pre-training → post-training)
  • Bias and fairness risk detection across user segments
  • False positive / false negative impact analysis
  • Model explainability using SHAP-based interpretation
  • Regulatory compliance mapping and risk classification

Regulatory & Governance Frameworks

The audit is aligned with internationally recognized AI governance standards and local regulatory expectations.

NIST AI RMF EU AI Act Bank of Ghana CISD

Key Findings

  • Strong fraud detection capability across high-value transactions
  • Elevated false positives in mid-balance users
  • Bias linked to balance-based behavioral features
  • Fairness risk classified as material
